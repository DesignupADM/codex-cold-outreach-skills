# Cold Outreach Research Notes

This file captures the research set used to strengthen the `cold-outreach-campaign` skill on May 19, 2026.

## Main patterns extracted

Across the linked articles, the most repeated themes were:

- Authentication and deliverability hygiene come before copy polish
- Warm-up and conservative send-volume ramps reduce inbox-placement risk
- List verification is mandatory, with bounce rate kept below a low threshold
- Short, signal-based subject lines and opening lines outperform generic intros
- Each follow-up should add new value rather than repeat the first email
- Reply quality matters more than vanity opens when judging campaign health
- Launch readiness includes rendering checks, seed tests, suppression, and opt-out logic

## How those insights changed the skill

The skill now includes:

- A deliverability and preflight reference
- A dedicated opening-lines reference
- A measurement and benchmark reference
- Launch-ready workflow steps, not just copywriting steps
