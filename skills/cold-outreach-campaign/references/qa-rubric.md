# QA Rubric

Use this reference to evaluate campaign quality before final delivery.

## Score each category from 1 to 5

### Relevance

- Is the email clearly tied to the target role or company context?
- Does personalization support the angle instead of sitting on top of it?

### Clarity

- Can the prospect understand the point in one read?
- Is the offer concrete rather than vague?

### Copy strength

- Is the first sentence strong?
- Is the body concise?
- Are filler and weak transitions removed?

### Proof

- Is there a believable reason to trust the sender?
- Are claims concrete enough to sound real?

### CTA quality

- Is the ask easy to answer?
- Is there only one primary ask?

### Tone

- Does it sound human and commercially aware?
- Is the intensity appropriate for the audience?

### Sequence quality

- Do the touches evolve logically?
- Is each email distinct enough to justify sending?

## Fast revision guide

If the draft scores low on:

- Relevance: sharpen the angle, not just the personalization
- Clarity: reduce ideas and simplify the offer
- Copy strength: cut the intro and compress the body
- Proof: replace adjectives with specifics
- CTA quality: lower the ask or make it easier to answer
- Tone: remove hype and over-explanation

## Final launch checklist

- Subject line matches the body
- First line creates a reason to continue
- Offer is visible without effort
- Proof is present but not bloated
- CTA is simple
- No deceptive tactics or fake familiarity
- No sentence feels like template filler
